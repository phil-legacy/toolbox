# Phil's Toolbox · Industrial Sciences

Free teaching tools for industrial sciences (automation, systems, simulation), by Philippe RAYMOND.

Official address: https://phil-legacy.github.io/toolbox/

- **Flowchart to ST v1.2**: draw a structured flowchart, get Structured Text, simulate it like a PLC task and record timing diagrams.
- **Grafcet to ST v1.0**: draw grafcets (IEC 60848) with forcing orders and macro-steps, check them, simulate them scan by scan, and get Structured Text (cf / X method).
- **Fluid Power v0.7.0 (beta)**: draw pneumatic or hydraulic circuits (ISO 1219 symbols) and watch them run: pressures, flows, cylinders under load, sequences, displacement-step diagram, pressure curves and oil heating; 27 examples (simplified physics, made for learning).
- **SysML Starter v0.7.1 (beta)**: describe a machine by answering simple questions and get the six SysML v1 diagrams (req, bdd, ibd, uc, stm, sd), with explanations, checks, step-by-step examples and PDF export (French / English, computer or tablet).
- **Cyber Workshop v1.0 (beta)**: industrial cybersecurity, hands-on: hunt the flaws of a workshop, play attack scenarios against your measures, replay eight real cases, run an incident drill and learn the cybersecurity side of the Machinery Regulation (EU) 2023/1230 (French / English, works offline). Beta: complete and audited, waiting for its first classroom sessions.

Digital culture (outside industrial sciences, in French):

- **Cyber Réflexes v1.6.0**: a digital safety course in 15 modules (including image rights, cyberbullying and email use at work), with situations to solve, three interactive labs, eleven “What to do if…?” sheets (including a data breach), a glossary and a printable report; works offline, no data collected.

What's new in each version: see [CHANGELOG.md](CHANGELOG.md).

Use these tools to learn and prepare; always validate your program in your PLC software (CODESYS…).
