Phil's Toolbox

Industrial Sciences

Free teaching tools for industrial sciences: automation, systems, simulation. They run in your browser, with nothing to install, on a computer, a tablet or a phone.

Tools

Flowchart to STv1.2

Draw a structured flowchart, get the Structured Text (Head and Body) and simulate it like a PLC task: step by step, scan by scan or in real time, with a watchdog, and record timing diagrams of the variables.

Open the tool →

Grafcet to STv1.0

Draw one or several grafcets (IEC 60848) with forcing orders and macro-steps, check them, simulate them scan by scan like a PLC, and get the Structured Text with the cf / X method. Drawing needs a computer; on a phone you can read and simulate.

Open the tool →
